## University

I dropped out before finishing the first semester.
I failed two main subjects which left me with too little credit to pass the semester.

I was taught to believe that getting a degree is the most important thing ever and failing that made me feel like a complete failure.

## More attempts

The obvious move was to try to "fix" things by getting back into university.

First I applied for a degree in film-making, didn't pass the entry exams.

After that I applied for the same IT degree that I failed before. And I actually passed the entry exams! I remember feeling very proud of myself because of that.

But after a deep look within myself, I realized that university might not be the best path for me.

## Looking for a job

Before the semester started I was looking around for a possible job.
I think I wrote to ~6 companies and only one responded. And they responded positively!

The hiring process was completely smooth ordinary so I won't go into any details here.

And so I got my first job!

## Work life

Starting work was a totally great decision. I was so happy that I had authority over my free time.
No homework, no studying, it was great!
I got used to the daily routine of driving to work, working, driving back home and having a few hours of free time fairly quickly.

All the comforts of having my own income and almost no responsibilities because I lived with my parents.
This was the closest to a perfect life one could imagine.

## Moving out

I started dating my current girlfriend mid-November 2023.
We stayed with my parents for a few months but we quickly realized that's not viable long-term.

We moved out some time in April 2024.

This is where the perfect life ended.

## Living on your own

I didn't expect much to change. I'd go to work, go back home, have some free time, do some chores and go to sleep.

But a lot has changed. Those chores happen to take up a lot more free time than I expected.
And living together with another person sure has a learning curve, but we're both confident that we'll be able to figure it out.

## And that's it!

I hope I'll be able to provide more life updates as they happen instead of making these types of loooong summary posts.
