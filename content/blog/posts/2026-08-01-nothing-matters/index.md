### the concept

I got sick and I started binge-watching For All Mankind. I downed the whole first season in a single day. I think you can guess how I feel based just on that...

I can't recommend the show enough. I have to admit, I went into it thinking I'll be getting some nice sci-fi that just so happened to begin in history and that the history part of it is only going to be a very small portion. But I'm well into the second season now and it's still in the past.

Anyway, there was a character moment that happened in Season 2 that just connected with me on some deep level. I don't want to spoil anything so I'll keep it vague and simple.

The basic thing that happened was that this one character basically had everything going for them. They've done amazing things in the past, even their current life is going very well. And yet they're still not happy... Just this simple realization sent me crying. It just made me wonder if we can ever trust ourselves as the judges of our life. Happiness is short-lived, it happens in the moment. The rest of our life is what we make it I guess...
